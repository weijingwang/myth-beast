# I Thought Raising a Mythical Beast Would Be Easy, but Somehow I’ve Become the Laughingstock of the Kingdom

A visual novel made for PyWeek 42 (theme: *Borrowed Time*) by Team Chinkaraoshiman. It is about the young lady who, wishing for high social standing, is met with a mythical beast and now both are living on borrowed time.


## About

> Every corner of the Kingdom knows my family has captured the mythical beast of the Highlands.
>
> "Every corner of the kingdom shall recognize the greatness of the House of Claire!"
>
> But... Wait... the mythical beast is a goat?! And he is severely brain damaged!!
>
> The doctor says he is living on "Borrowed Time".
>
> Forget the beast, my reputation is on "Borrowed Time"!!

## Requirements
We used ```pygame-ce 2.5.5``` and ```Python 3.12.4```.

Install:

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

or simply `pip install pygame-ce`.

## How to play

```
python main.py
```

Click, or press Space or Enter, to advance the story. Click a button to make a choice. There are 4 endings.
